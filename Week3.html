About

Student Daily Schedule: plan, track, and be notified.

The Problem

Students often keep class times, study sessions, and school commitments in separate notes, chat apps, paper planners, or memory. It is hard to see the whole day in one place, so activities get missed or remembered at the last minute.

Our Solution

One simple dashboard to add, view, edit, and remove schedule entries, with an in-app reminder and a browser notification minutes before an activity starts.

Main Features

  • Dashboard for today's activities and the next schedule
  • Date, start/end time, location, and notes
  • Minute mark advance alert
  • Search by title or location
  • Customizable alert sound and volume

Who is it for?

Applicable for students including teachers and parents, who wants to organize a daily schedule.
